## What "DDoS Protected" Actually Has to Mean in 2026

Not all protection is the same, and the gap between a real scrubbing infrastructure and a marketing bullet point is enormous. Here's what separates the two:

- **Null-routing is not protection.** When most basic providers get hit, they black-hole your IP. Your server survives. Your users see nothing. That's not protecting you — that's protecting their network from you.
- **Software-based filtering consumes your own CPU and RAM**, meaning a sustained attack can exhaust your server's resources before traffic even gets scrubbed.
- **Network-level mitigation** happens at the data center's edge. Malicious packets are dropped before they enter the internal network at all. Your VPS resources stay devoted to your actual application.

Real mitigation handles the full menu: UDP floods, TCP SYN floods, ICMP floods, HTTP floods, DNS amplification, NTP amplification, Slowloris, MemCached reflection — the whole attack library. It operates at multi-gigabit capacity, not 1–5 Gbps "best effort" thresholds that saturate the moment a mid-tier botnet wakes up.
